The Order

The Order is for people willing to commit, work hard, sacrifice, and join a new family — a brotherhood, a tribe. It is not for the soft, the lazy, or for people who want to pretend. Fantasy made real, not reality made fantasy.

The shape of the path

You begin as an initiate and work your way toward peer rank — the vocation of Sentinel or Steward. Knighthood exists for those who exemplify the Order in their vocation and service. It is not easy, and not everyone achieves it.

Everyone meets a baseline standard of fitness, martial prowess, and skills. Your vocation determines your specializations and your focus.
