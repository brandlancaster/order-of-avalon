The Order of Avalon is a modern order of knights who train in unarmed combat, knightly arms, modern arms, and service — then put that training to work helping and protecting people.

The Order of Avalon is a modern order of knights. Not honorary titles for actors and politicians — real knights who train, who wear the armor, who put boots on the ground. We blend Arthurian ideals with martial discipline and a knightly brotherhood. Members train in unarmed combat, knightly arms, modern arms, and service. We field teams to help and protect. Advancement is earned through training, service, and ceremony. It’s selective. Standards are real. Avalon is the dream. The Order is how you live it.
