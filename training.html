Training and Standards

Disciplines

Members train in four disciplines.

  • Unarmed combat
  • Knightly arms
  • Modern arms
  • Service

Fitness

  • To qualify as an Initiate you must pass a baseline physical fitness test (score of 50).
  • To advance to novice rank in either vocation you must pass the membership minimum (score of 60).
  • Members retest yearly.
